# eli5

Explain the user's requested topic like they know nothing about it, using an HTML artifact with big pictures and few words.

## Requirements

- **Bilingual（双语解释）**: Explain using both English and Chinese.
- **Line-by-Line Format（一行英文一行中文）**: Present every line/sentence in English followed immediately by its Chinese translation on the next line.
- **Simple & Visual**: Keep the explanations dead-simple and intuitive with large visual illustrations and minimal text.
